Zova Privacy Policy

Publisher: Vahid Rahmani · Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy describes how Zova processes information when you use the extension.

Overview

Zova is an AI classroom assistant for user-initiated tab-audio transcription, translation, classroom assistance and local lesson storage.

Information Zova Processes

Depending on the features you use, Zova may process classroom tab audio, transcripts, translations, Smart Notes, saved classes, AI prompts and your Gemini API key.

Classroom Tab Audio

When you press Start, Zova captures audio from the selected browser tab and streams it to Google Gemini. Raw audio is not intentionally stored as a file by Zova.

Transcripts and Translations

Generated transcripts and translations are displayed and may be saved locally when you save a class.

Google Gemini

User-initiated audio and bounded classroom context may be sent to Google Gemini. Google’s terms and privacy policy apply.

Google AI Mode

When you explicitly choose Explain, Answer or Summary, Zova opens Google AI Mode and submits a bounded classroom-derived query. Your Gemini API key is not sent to Google AI Mode.

Gemini API Key

Your key is stored locally in Chrome extension storage and is not included in exported class backups.

Local Storage and Saved Classes

Settings, transcripts, translations, Smart Notes and Clean Study Files are stored locally until you delete them or clear extension data.

Backup and Export

Backups are manually requested and contain saved class data, not your Gemini API key.

Website Access

Zova may request optional HTTP/HTTPS access when you start a mode that requires it.

Microphone Permission

Zova may request microphone permission for compatibility or verification. Normal Smart Class tab capture does not require physical microphone input.

Data Sharing

Zova shares information with Google only when needed for a user-initiated feature. Zova does not sell user data, use classroom data for personalized advertising or create advertising profiles.

Data Retention

Saved classes remain locally until deleted. Remote retention is governed by applicable Google service terms.

User Control and Deletion

You can stop sessions, delete classes, remove your API key and avoid optional AI actions.

Security

Zova limits API-key handling to its extension/API path and excludes the key from backups.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when functionality or legal requirements change. Contact Info.rivo24@gmail.com.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
